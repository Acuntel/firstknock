# FirstKnock — Market Research (August 2026)

Compiled from a sourced research sweep on 2026-08-09. Numbers below carry their sources in the underlying research; vendor-claimed figures are marked. This doc answers four questions: how do you actually reach motivated sellers, who do operators already pay, how do we beat each of them, and who exactly is our buyer.

## 1. Reaching motivated home sellers: what works in 2026

Ranked by cost per closed deal, with the current legal reality baked in:

| Channel                    | Cost per closed deal                       | Response/contact rate                                                                 | Trend and risk                                                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cold calling               | $1,000-2,000 all-in                        | ~8% contact rate; ~125 dials per qualified lead on FRESH lists, 400-600 on stale ones | Cheapest but saturating fast; mini-TCPA states (incl. MICHIGAN, FL, OK, WA) carry private lawsuit rights |
| Direct mail                | $2,500-5,000                               | 0.5-3% response on targeted distress lists                                            | Safest legally (zero TCPA exposure); postage +~5%/yr two years running                                   |
| Attorney/probate referrals | Low cash cost, high relationship cost      | Highest-trust channel; probate leads $65-180 each                                     | Underused, slow to build                                                                                 |
| PPC / Google Ads           | ~$5,000-20,000 (derived)                   | $60-500+ per lead depending on metro; landing pages convert 5-15%                     | Highest intent, steadily more expensive                                                                  |
| SEO / content sites        | Slow-build                                 | ~8.5% site conversion (vendor claim)                                                  | 6-12 months to rank; compounding once ranked                                                             |
| Cold SMS                   | n/a                                        | 5-12% when it works                                                                   | Effectively DEAD for prospecting in 2026: A2P registration, carrier filtering, TCPA suits +95% in 2025   |
| Door knocking              | Unquantified (no credible 2026 benchmarks) | Adjacent stat: 78% of owners hire the first professional to respond after a loss      | Free, unscalable, works best event-triggered                                                             |

**The strategic read for FirstKnock:** the two best-performing legal-safe channels (mail, door) and the cheapest one (calling, where state law allows) all share one lever: LIST QUALITY AND TIMING. The dials-per-lead number triples on stale lists. Every channel gets cheaper when the lead is hours old instead of weeks old and nobody else has it yet. FirstKnock is not a channel; it is the timing layer that makes the channels work, and its built-in actions (letters, door scripts, email drafts) are deliberately the low-liability set. Michigan's mini-TCPA specifically validates our mail/door/email-first design in a launch state.

**Fastest single lead source found in the whole sweep (agent-side):** expired listings convert 43% to appointment (REDX study of 2.7M leads), for a future agent-facing preset.

### The honest strategic correction

"Same-day county data" is REAL as an edge against the PropStream/Batch aggregator layer (their lag is documented), but it is now TABLE STAKES among 2025-2026 entrants: PropertyRadar, DistressIQ, and Goliath all market fresh polling. Nobody, however, combines all four of: (1) same-day event polling, (2) built-in low-liability outreach, (3) multi-vertical presets beyond REI, (4) fire + permits + tax + foreclosure + HOA in ONE digest. That combination is the moat, and the county-adapter library plus vertical packaging is what a well-funded competitor would have to grind through to copy. Pitch accordingly: lead with "know first, act in one click, for YOUR trade," not with data freshness alone.

## 3. Target audience: top people, top industries, top use cases

### Ranked by willingness to pay (value per won job × proven current spend)

1. **Restoration owners/ops managers** — 62,582 US companies (+4.3% YoY). Avg fire job ~$27,000; already paying $697-1,497/mo for fire alerts ALONE and up to $2,250 per exclusive lead. One saved job pays for FirstKnock for 4+ years. Best-fit persona: independent or franchise operator, 10-50 employees, chasing large-loss commercial.
2. **Small real-estate investors/flippers/wholesalers** — investors bought 33-34% of US homes in 2025; ~300K flips/yr; ~$15K net per flip; 52% spend under $1K/mo on marketing (our $149-299 tiers fit), established wholesalers ~$5K/mo. Biggest population, most tool-saturated; we win the ones burned by stale aggregator data.
3. **Storm/retail roofers** — 108,598 US companies (+2.6%). $11.5-17.6K average replacement; currently bleeding ~$1,400 per booked job on a decaying Angi. Permit volume is flat-to-down nationally, so this vertical's signal is weaker in 2026; sell the Angi-escape story, not volume.
4. **Foreclosure-defense / HOA / creditor law firms** — count unavailable, but 373,000 community associations (record high) need counsel and foreclosure filings are surging. Lowest per-case value ($1,500-5K flat or retainer + monthly) but recurring caseloads and ZERO SaaS competition. Highest strategic value per dollar of adapter work.

## 4. Market context 2026 (the tailwind)

- Foreclosure filings 1H 2026: 227,548 properties, +21% YoY, +28% vs 2024; timelines fastest since 2013 (563 days), which shortens lead shelf-life and rewards freshness.
- Tax delinquency 5.1% YTD 2025 (7-year high); property taxes +27% since 2019.
- Illinois is a top-5 foreclosure state (Chicago a top REO metro); Michigan is top-5 in REOs with a large separate county tax-foreclosure pipeline (Wayne County auctions every Sept-Oct). Both launch states are in the top distress tier; the launch map matches the wave.
- Watch-outs: permits flat-to-down (roofing signal weaker); normal home-sale volume depressed; Michigan mini-TCPA (mail/email-first there, no cold-call product features in V1).
